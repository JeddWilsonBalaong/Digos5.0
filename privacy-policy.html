Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information (e.g., name, address, social security number)
  • Financial information (e.g., account balances, transaction history)
  • Contact information (e.g., phone number, email address)

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide you with banking services and products
  • To process transactions and account management
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Our affiliates and service providers to facilitate banking operations
  • Government authorities as required by law
  • Third parties with your consent

Protecting Your Information

We maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information.

Your Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Correct inaccuracies in your information
  • Opt out of certain data sharing practices
