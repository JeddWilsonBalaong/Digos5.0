Who Are We?

Your Trusted Financial Partner in Digos

Rural Bank of Digos is a community-focused financial institution dedicated to serving the banking needs of individuals, families, and businesses in Digos City and surrounding areas. Since our establishment, we have been committed to providing personalized banking services that foster economic growth and financial prosperity in our community.

As a rural bank, we understand the unique financial challenges and opportunities that exist in our local market. Our team of experienced professionals combines traditional banking values with modern financial solutions to deliver exceptional service to every customer who walks through our doors.

Our Journey

Bank History

Decades of serving the Digos community

1999

Bank Establishment

Rural Bank of Digos was founded with a vision to serve the financial needs of the growing Digos community. Starting with a small team of dedicated professionals and a commitment to personalized service.

2003

First Branch Expansion

Opened our first branch office in downtown Digos, significantly expanding our reach and capacity to serve more customers with improved accessibility and convenience.

2008

Digital Banking Launch

Introduced our first digital banking services, including online account access and electronic fund transfers, bringing modern convenience to our rural banking services.

2012

Regional Recognition

Received recognition as "Outstanding Rural Bank" from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas for our contribution to financial inclusion and community development.

2018

Mobile Banking Innovation

Launched our comprehensive mobile banking application, enabling customers to access full banking services from their smartphones, setting new standards for rural banking technology.

2024

Growth Milestone

Achieved significant growth milestones with over ₱2 billion in assets under management and 10,000+ satisfied customers, reinforcing our position as a leading rural bank in the region.
